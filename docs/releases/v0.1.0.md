This was the first intentionally early public release of Gen2 Clean UI: a
modular, fail-open presentation layer for Pokémon Gold that can be updated as
an ordinary launcher mod.

## Included baseline

- A production Gen II battle presentation for stable wild and trainer battle
  menus, move selection, message frames, touch action geometry, palette-aware
  sprites, and metadata-rich status cards.
- Status-card coverage for level, HP, gender where applicable, conditions,
  wild catch state, and the player's experience bar.
- Responsive layout coverage from phone-sized portrait and landscape views
  through desktop, ultrawide, 4K, and 5K displays.
- Exact-contract presenters for the audited Pokegear/Map Radio,
  services/commerce, mail, specialty, and Hall of Fame families, plus the
  production Gallery conversion.
- A fail-open boundary that intentionally leaves battle-owned child stacks,
  transitions, and animations native until their contracts are ready.
- Headless scaffold, core-lock, archive, and contract validation in GitHub
  Actions, with a deterministic updater-ready ZIP.
